- **Captions** are always centered, white with a thin outline and no background.
- **Taps on the video:** one tap shows or hides the controls; a double tap on the left or right goes back or forward 10 s, and more taps add up ("+30 s"); a double tap in the middle pauses.
- **Skip ahead** now uses YouTube's own "most replayed" graph to jump over the parts most people skip, such as sponsor segments, and marks them on the timeline.
- **Rotation:** the player turns by itself to fit the video, and a button switches between landscape and portrait.
- Phones and tablets start at 1080p; 4K first stays on TV.
- Three columns on tablets in portrait, and History as rows like a playlist.
- Explore builds itself from topics found in your watch history.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/JohanKRS/beamtube/compare/v0.5.0...v0.5.1
