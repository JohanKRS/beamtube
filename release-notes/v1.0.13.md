- **TV: back at the top after a while away.** When you come back to BeamTube after some time and the list has refreshed, the tab now opens at the top with the first video selected, instead of pulling the focus down to where an old video used to be. Switching tabs, turning the screen and changing the language still keep your place.
- **Recommended shows the channel again.** Some Recommended videos showed a label like "4K" or "New" where the channel name goes, or no channel at all. BeamTube now takes the channel from the video's info line, so Recommended looks like the other tabs: the channel on top, then views and date. On the phone and tablet, the channel photo opens the channel again. Lists saved before the update are fixed as soon as they open.
- **View counts in more languages.** Numbers with special spaces and "Sin vistas" in Spanish are now read correctly, so those videos keep their views and date.
- **A faster, lighter app.** Lists, saved data and settings are now read and written in the background, saving a video redraws only its own card, and thumbnails fetched ahead are only downloaded, not decoded, until they show up on screen. Unused resources are left out of the APK, and the startup profile now covers the first screen and the player, so the app opens faster.
- **Videos open sooner.** The video's stream is prepared in the background while the saved position loads at the same time, and the end screen is only built when a video ends. Regular videos no longer wake the app every second; only live streams do.
- **Phones and tablets with Android 11 or older.** The bars now draw their background directly instead of copying the whole screen on every frame, which makes scrolling lighter on older devices. They look the same.
- **Skip spots and chapters use less memory.** The page they come from is now read as it downloads, instead of being loaded whole into memory first.
