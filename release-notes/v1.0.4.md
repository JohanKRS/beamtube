- **TV Settings now explain the YouTube button on the remote.** A new "YouTube button on the remote" section says whether BeamTube's Accessibility service is on, whether the button has been learned yet, and opens Android's Accessibility settings directly, so you no longer have to find that screen yourself. If a TV doesn't have it, the general settings open instead. The same service lets sleep mode turn off the TV.
- **A bigger logo on the TV home screen.** The light, the beam and the name now fill more of the BeamTube card, instead of sitting small in the middle.
