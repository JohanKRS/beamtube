A new look, plus a few things that make the lists quicker to use.

- Graphite background, a warm amber light on what matters (the open tab, progress, focus on TV) and a new font.
- On phones and tablets, glass bars from edge to edge, with the content scrolling underneath (blurred on Android 12 and newer).
- Tablets: three columns in portrait, and a rail of icons on the left in landscape.
- Long-press a thumbnail to save it to Watch later, or to remove it. A small marker shows what's already saved.
- On TV, holding OK on a video opens a menu with Save and Go to channel.
- The avatar and channel name under each video open the channel.
- Pull down to refresh a list. On TV, hold OK on a tab.
- A clearer "Subscribed" state on playlist pages, and a red icon color.
