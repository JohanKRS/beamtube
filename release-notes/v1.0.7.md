- **Several users, with a quick switch.** Everyone in the house can sign in with their own YouTube account and keep their own topics, blocked channels, settings, saved lists and where each video stopped. Your photo at the top opens a menu to switch users, add an account or, on the phone, open Settings; on the TV it's at the end of the tabs, or in the corner of the title with the side menu. The Users group at the top of Settings does the same. BeamTube opens on the last user, with no picker when it starts. A Google account with brand channels becomes one user per channel, and the account that was already signed in keeps everything it had.
- **Guest.** Use BeamTube without an account, with only Recommended and Search: nothing goes to any account, and the device doesn't remember where you stopped in each video. "Use without an account" on the sign-in screen opens it, and the photo menu always has Guest.
- **Signing out keeps your settings.** Sign out removes the account and its saved lists from the device, but its topics, blocked channels and other settings stay, so they're back if the same account signs in again. The language, icon color, accent color, YouTube button and TV layout stay the same for everyone on the device.
- **A side menu on the TV, if you prefer it.** In Settings > Layout, the TV can now use the tablet layout: the tab icons in a menu on the left, with Settings at the bottom, and the name of the open tab on top. Moving through the menu changes the page, OK on the open tab reloads it, and Back from a list takes you to the menu. Tabs on top are still the default.
- **A darker background on the TV.** The TV background is now near-black, with cards and menus darkened to match. TV screens showed the old dark gray much lighter than phones do. Phones and tablets don't change.
- **The loading screen takes the colors of the video.** While a video loads, its thumbnail now fills the whole screen, heavily blurred, so only its colors show. Before, it sat in the middle with black around it, and some thumbnails brought black bars of their own. When the video starts, it fades in from those colors.
- **A quick animation when the player opens and closes.** On the TV and the phone, the player grows into place over the list when you open a video and shrinks away when you go back. It takes a fifth of a second, so it adds a bit of life without making you wait.
- **Drag the video down to close it.** On the phone and tablet, put a finger anywhere on the video and drag it down: it follows your finger and the player closes when you let go. Let go early and it goes back. Handy if you hide the navigation bar and its gesture handle. Taps and double taps work as before.
- **A website.** BeamTube now has a short tour at [beamtube.johankrs.com](https://beamtube.johankrs.com/), in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
