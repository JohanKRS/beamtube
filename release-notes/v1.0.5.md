- **Topic suggestions stay where they are.** On the topics screen (first launch, or Settings > Topics > "Choose from suggestions"), picking a suggestion used to make it wider, pushing the next ones down to another line, so the list kept shifting as you picked. Now every suggestion keeps its size whether it's picked or not, on the TV and on the phone.
- **Fuller lines of suggestions.** Each line now reaches the right edge, with the extra space shared among its suggestions, instead of leaving a gap on the right. The last line stays as it is.
