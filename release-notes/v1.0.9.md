- **Pick the language of the captions.** The captions button now opens a small panel, like the quality one, with Off and up to three choices: the app's language, the video's original language and English. When the original language is the app's or English, it shows up only once. If the video has no captions in the language you pick, YouTube translates them automatically from the original captions. Your choice carries over to the next videos: with Original, each video shows captions in its own language. Titles and audio stay in the original language, as before.
- **A lighter blur while a video loads.** The thumbnail behind the loading circle, and behind the end screen, is only lightly blurred now, so you can tell which video it is.
- **Drag to close, without moving the video.** On phones and tablets, pulling down on the video no longer slides it down: it stays put, blurring and darkening as you pull, and closes when you let go. Let go early and it comes back.
- **The end screen shows up with the brightness turned down too.** Only a limit in Then play or Turn off keeps the video stopped at the end, as before.
- **No more bright strip under a dimmed video.** On some TVs, a strip at the bottom of the video stayed at full brightness with the brightness turned down. The 1.0.8 fix missed the cause; this one covers the whole video.
- **Subscribed playlists have their own screen** in Settings > Playlists, like Your topics, so Settings no longer grows with the list.
- **TV side menu:** going from the list to the menu and back now lands on the same video, instead of the one above it. The highlight lines up with the top of the thumbnails again, as in 1.0.7, while the menu keeps its smaller size from 1.0.8.
