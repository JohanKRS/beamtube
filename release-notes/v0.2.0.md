- **Subscriptions is the home screen.** No Shorts, and no live streams unless you turn on "Show live streams" in Settings.
- **The player:** captions on and off with one tap, like and dislike, subscribe to the channel, and a "Skip ahead" button that jumps to the most watched part.
- **Subscribe to playlists.** Open a playlist from another channel and tap Subscribe; new videos added to it show up in Subscriptions (unless you already follow that channel).
- **Settings:** live streams, what to do with links from other apps (no history, save to history, or ask every time), your subscribed playlists and your account.
- On TV, everything works with the D-pad: tabs on top, Back returns to the tabs.
