- **Pick the background color.** Settings > Background now has Graphite, Near black and Pure black (OLED) on every device. Pure black switches off the pixels of OLED screens; cards and bars stay visible on it. The TV still starts on near black and the phone and tablet on graphite.
- **Your topics on a screen of their own.** In Settings > Topics, "Choose from suggestions" and the new "Your topics" each open their own screen, with how many topics you have in each list. "Your topics" holds See more of and See less of, where you edit, remove and add topics, so Settings no longer gets long.
- **Explore filters behind a button.** On the phone and tablet, Explore opens without the filters, lined up with the other tabs. The button next to "Explore" shows and hides them, and lights up in your accent color when a filter is on. The filter chips now run from edge to edge, like in Search. The TV doesn't change.
- **Guest is now Search only.** YouTube doesn't recommend anything without an account, so Recommended was always empty for the guest. Guest now opens straight on Search.
- **A softer blur, and at the end of a video too.** The blurred thumbnail behind the loading screen is lighter, so you can make out its shapes and colors. The end screen now sits over the same blurred thumbnail instead of black.
- **Dragging a video down closes it the way it opened.** On the phone and tablet, when you let go, the blurred thumbnail fades back over the video and the player shrinks away, like going back.
- **Subscribe on the channel tabs row.** On a phone, a channel's Subscribe button now sits on the row of Videos and Playlists, the same size, on the right.
- **TV player controls.** Back with the controls on screen now only hides them; Back again leaves the video. The bottom row of buttons wraps around: Left on Play goes to the sleep button, and Right on the sleep button goes back to Play. OK on the sleep button only opens and closes its box and keeps the focus there; Up goes into the box, and Down from its bottom row comes back to the sleep button instead of the captions.
- **TV side menu.** The side menu is a little narrower, with smaller icons, and its first icon lines up with the top of the first row of thumbnails (on the tablet's side menu too). Going from a list to the side menu and back now returns to the same video.
- **TV search field.** The search field on the TV now spans the full width of the page.
- **Fixed:** with a low sleep brightness, the second video and the ones after it showed a bright strip at the bottom of the screen.
- **Fixed:** on some TVs, "Open Accessibility settings" in Settings said that no app could do that. It now opens the settings app directly.
