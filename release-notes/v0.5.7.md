A big polish release.

- **The player is more compact**, with a bottom bar from edge to edge. On TV, like, dislike, Save and Subscribe moved into it.
- **No more Skip ahead button:** seeking forward inside a part most people skip jumps straight to its end, with a quick "Jumped ahead".
- **Chapters** on the timeline, the current chapter's name, and a thumbnail preview while you scrub.
- **Pick a highlight color** in Settings.
- **Reorder by press and drag**; swipe right to move a video to the top.
- Settings grouped in cards, cleaner video cards, empty placeholder cards while loading, and no more glow around indicators.
- The Explore explanation shows only the first time.
- On TV, holding OK for 0.3 s is enough to open the menu.
- **Faster:** videos start sooner, lists scroll more smoothly, thumbnails load over HTTP/2 (HTTP/3 on Android 14), and the APK is optimized with R8.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/JohanKRS/beamtube/compare/v0.5.6...v0.5.7
