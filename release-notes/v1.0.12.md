- **Fixed: 1.0.11 closed right after opening.** Recommended, which now loads in the background right after Subscriptions, read one of its values before it existed, and the app closed every time it opened, on the TV and on the phone. It opens normally again, with everything 1.0.11 brought.
- **Clearer steps for the YouTube button (TV).** Settings > YouTube button on the remote now lists what to do in Accessibility, in order: open it, scroll to the end of the list, select BeamTube and turn it on, then press the YouTube button once.
- **"Open Accessibility settings" skips fake shortcuts.** Some TVs list an empty placeholder for that screen, which only showed "No app can perform this action". BeamTube now skips it and opens the real Accessibility settings.
- **TCL TVs: why the button stops after an update.** TCL doesn't let apps start on their own, so after BeamTube updates or the TV restarts, the YouTube button only comes back once you open BeamTube. Settings now says so on those TVs, and the README has the ADB command to allow auto-start.
- **Fixed: videos with only automatic captions had none.** The automatic captions were mistaken for a translation and left out, so those videos showed no captions, and "Original" couldn't tell the video's language.
- **Fixed: captions sometimes didn't show until you skipped.** On some TVs the first caption download failed without an answer, and the video stayed without captions until a seek asked again. BeamTube now tries once more right away.
- **Captions when YouTube refuses a translation.** YouTube has been refusing its automatic caption translations to apps other than its own. When the caption language you picked needs one and it doesn't come, BeamTube now shows the video's own captions (the original language first, then English) instead of nothing.
- **TV: the seek preview opens in place.** Pressing left or right with the controls hidden opened the first preview in the wrong spot and too big. It now opens centered on the bar, the right size, from the first press.
- **Up next cards stay aligned.** On the end screen, every suggestion now has two lines for the title and one for the channel, so the row no longer jumps as you scroll, and the suggestions from the video page show their channel too.
- **Lighter video loading.** The video's manifest no longer carries a track for every caption and translation (often more than a hundred), so it's smaller and the player only sees the caption you picked.
