The very first build. Back then the app was called Doorglow; it became BeamTube in 1.0.

- Sign in with a code on youtube.com/activate, the same way on phones and TVs.
- Your playlists, including the real Watch later.
- Videos play in the best quality available, always with the original audio track, never an automatic dub.
- YouTube links opened from other apps (WhatsApp, the browser, Share) play in the app and are not saved to your watch history.
- Every version is published here as a signed APK, so you can follow updates with Obtainium.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/JohanKRS/beamtube/commits/v0.1.0
