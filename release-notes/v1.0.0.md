BeamTube 1.0 is here. It brings together everything since 0.6.2 (versions 0.6.3, 0.7.0 and 0.7.1 were never published on their own), and a few things just for 1.0.

> **Coming from Doorglow?** The app has a new name and a new app ID, so Android installs BeamTube as a separate app. Add it to Obtainium again, sign in, pick your topics and redo your device settings (blocked channels, Save playlist, colors). Your subscribed playlists live in your YouTube account and come back on their own. On TV, turn on BeamTube's Accessibility service again. Then uninstall Doorglow.

## New in 1.0

- **Pick your topics on first launch.** A new screen offers more than 40 topics to see more of (documentaries, history, engineering, sleep and relaxing videos…) and to see less of (news, gaming, celebrity gossip…). There are no preset topics anymore: you start from what you choose, and you can come back any time in Settings > Topics.
- **English, Portuguese and Spanish.** The app follows your device's language, or you can choose one in Settings > Language.
- **When a video ends,** BeamTube shows a few suggestions (the next one from the list you came from, one from the same channel, and related videos that follow your topics) with Back already focused. If you don't pick one, it goes back to your list after 15 seconds. Choose what happens in Settings > When a video ends: show suggestions, go back, play a suggestion, or stay on the video. Playlists still follow the player's "Then play", and with the sleep mode on, the video just stops (or the TV turns off) as before.
- On TV, **Settings use the full width** of the screen, and **the tabs always fit on one line** next to the logo, whatever the language or text size.
- **A README** that explains what BeamTube is and how it differs from the official app.
- **MIT License**, with the licenses of every library inside the app listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and shipped in the APK.

## A new name and a new look (from 0.7.0)

- Doorglow is now **BeamTube**, with a new icon (a beam of light through a gap), a new TV banner, and the full logo on the TV's top bar and the sign-in screen.
- The **Outfit** font everywhere, with a single type scale for TV, phones, tablets and the player.

## Faster, and never a black screen (from 0.7.1)

- From the moment the player opens, you see the video's thumbnail and a loading spinner until the first frame.
- The first video after opening the app starts much sooner: the engine gets ready in the background as soon as your lists appear.
- On TV, when the focus rests on a video for a couple of seconds, BeamTube gets it ready so it opens faster when you press OK.
- Captions no longer delay the start of a video.

## Keep listening (from 0.6.3)

- **The video keeps playing with the phone's screen off**, as audio only, and playlists keep going, with the sleep mode limits. Media controls appear on the lock screen and in the notification.
- **A floating mini player** when you leave the app while a video is playing (Android 8 and newer).
- **Subscriptions refresh by themselves** when you come back after 10 minutes or more, with the new videos on top.
- On TV, turning the TV off or leaving the app closes the player, so you come back to your list instead of a black screen. The video still resumes where you left off.
- The player shows how long ago the video was published.
- "Go to channel" works everywhere, including in Recommended.
- Video titles on TV cards are a little smaller, so more of the title fits.
