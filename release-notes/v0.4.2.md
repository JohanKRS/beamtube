- **A new player:** the title and channel at the top left, a glass pill with like, dislike, Save and Subscribe on the right, and a glass island at the bottom with the controls. The resolution and sleep panels open right above it.
- **Remove videos** from History and from your playlists by swiping left. On TV, hold OK on the video.
