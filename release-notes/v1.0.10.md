- **Caption language moved to Settings.** The captions button in the player is back to a simple on/off switch. Pick the language in Settings > Captions: the app's language, each video's original language or English. If a video has no captions in that language, YouTube translates them automatically; if that isn't possible, the original captions show. Titles and audio stay in the original language, as before.
- **A softer way back after dragging to close.** On phones and tablets, after you pull a video down to close it, the list now fades in from black instead of appearing all at once.
- **TV player: focus comes back to the same button.** Going up from the row of buttons to the timeline or the channel name, and then back down, lands on the button you left, instead of jumping to Subscribe.
- **Website:** the comparison table now says what BeamTube aims for on phone, tablet and TV: one app with a consistent look that doesn't keep changing.
