- **Blocking a channel now reaches your YouTube account.** Besides hiding the channel in BeamTube, blocking also sends YouTube's own "Don't recommend channel", so YouTube stops recommending it everywhere you're signed in: on your other devices, in the official app and on the website. YouTube only offers that request on recommended videos, so it goes out right away when the channel is in your Recommended, or the next time YouTube recommends it.
- **The blocked list belongs to each device, and BeamTube now says so.** The notice and Settings > Blocked channels explain it, and each blocked channel shows whether YouTube already got the request. Unblocking in BeamTube doesn't undo it on YouTube.
