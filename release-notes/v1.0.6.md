- **Reload a list from the tabs on the TV.** With the focus on the open tab (Subscriptions, Recommended, Explore, Playlists or History), press OK and the list reloads, showing empty cards until the new one arrives. Before, you had to hold OK on the tab, and Explore gave no sign that anything was happening. On the phone, pulling down still reloads, as before.
- **The YouTube button keeps working after an update.** Each time BeamTube was updated, Android stopped its Accessibility service and didn't always start it again, sometimes for hours, so the YouTube button on the remote opened YouTube until something else made Android look again. Now BeamTube checks after an update, when the TV starts and whenever you open it, and asks Android to bring the service back if it's on in the settings but not running.
- **Same focus on every row of the TV Settings.** The highlighted row used to grow past the edges of its card, so the last row of each group (or a group with a single row) looked different from the others, with the card's corners showing under it. Now the highlight stays inside the card and looks the same on every row.
